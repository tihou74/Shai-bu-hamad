The beginning

From the desert… to Doha

Shai Bu Hamad is not a cafe that opened in 2025. It is a habit centuries old, carried from the tent of hair into a shop in the city — the same tea, the same weave, and the same door that does not close.

01

The majlis — hospitality without a door

In the desert, hospitality kept no appointment and needed no invitation. The majlis was laid on the ground; whoever arrived sat down, and no one was asked their business before they were given something to drink. That same arrangement — long facing benches rather than separate tables — is what this place was built on.

02

The dallah — the first thing said

Before conversation, the cup is poured. The small measure is deliberate: it is not a serving meant to fill you, it is a signal to stay and be poured again. The dallah on our shelf is not an ornament — it is the first sentence the room says to its guest.

03

Sadu — a wall that is woven

Women wove it on the ground loom weft by weft, not for decoration but to be the wall of the house itself: it held off the sun and broke the wind. So when you see the pattern on our chairs and cushions, you are seeing the rest of that wall.

04

Now — the same majlis, in the city

We did not design a decor "in the Qatari style". We moved the original: the tea is poured in front of you rather than behind a door, the sadu is on the cushions rather than in a frame, and the sentence on our wall was not written by an advertising office — "A majlis with no heavy company". Two branches in Doha, and the same door standing open.

The story

A landmark in hospitality in the State of Qatar

Shai Bu Hamad was founded to serve a blend of Gulf heritage and Qatari taste.

It gives its guests a high standard of service and food that joins the authenticity of the Qatari kitchen to a contemporary Gulf hand. Warm rooms and comfortable seating, designed to reflect that cultural balance — a place for anyone looking for an exceptional table in the heart of Qatar.

The menu

Blends chosen by our own team

So the flavours meet properly and sit with our dishes — and for anyone who loves real Arabic coffee, our own blend is made for a careful palate.

Majlis tea

شاي المجلس

Poured hot into the glass, on a saucer carrying the name of the house.

This is the tea a majlis begins and ends with — clear, strong, no milk, served in its small glass to be drunk slowly and poured again. And our tea is made in front of you, not behind a door; that is why this place exists, not a line of advertising.

"Hayyak" — written on the cup

حياك

The welcome here is not only said at the door — it is written on the foam before the cup reaches the table.

And on the same tray: a dessert on a silver stand, and a napkin carrying the house drawing — a man in a ghutra holding his cup. Small details are what separate a place from the one next door, not the sign on the frontage.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where are the Shai Bu Hamad branches in Doha?

Two: West Walk in Al Waab — Al Sidra Street, and Gulf Mall in Al Gharrafa. Both are Qatari coffee shops with majlis seating.

What are the opening hours?

West Walk: every day from 7:00 am to 1:00 am, closed during Friday prayer.

Gulf Mall: Saturday to Thursday 7:00 am — 11:00 pm, and Friday from 1:00 pm to 11:00 pm.

How do I see the menu and prices?

The full menu with prices is on the menu page — and it is the same menu at both branches.

What do you serve?

Karak, Arabic coffee from our own blend, the majlis teapot, breakfast with regag and labneh, kabsa badawi, balaleet, pastries and cold drinks.

Can I sit and work there?

Yes — the room is built around long majlis seating rather than quick tables, and the sentence on our wall says as much: "A majlis with no heavy company".
